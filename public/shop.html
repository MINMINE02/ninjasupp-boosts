Good to know

What are credits?

Credits are the balance of your account wallet, in US dollars. Premium accounts use them to pay for the boosts they deliver; they never expire.

What does premium unlock?

Your own token stock, redeem keys, a branded redeem page, the joiner, the token checker and the API — everything for selling boosts under your own name.

Can I renew before premium ends?

Yes. Buying premium again adds the new days after your current end date, so nothing is lost.

How does Litecoin checkout work?

You get a one-time address and the exact amount. Send it, and this page updates by itself as soon as the payment is confirmed — usually within a few minutes.

I paid a little less by mistake

Small differences are fine. If the payment is a few cents short, the money stays in your wallet and you can finish the order from there.
